UNIT CONTROL / ILLUSTRATIVE

Defence Unit Management System

A Java Swing desktop app simulating a centralized control system communicating with Helicopter, Tank, and Submarine units.

  • Central controller with broadcast and private unit messaging.
  • Ammunition tracking and energy monitoring.
  • Single-click and continuous shooting with unit-specific operations.
  • Submarine oxygen monitoring and object-oriented implementation.
